## <a name="qr-code"></a>Your QR Code Pass — The Key to Playing

### What is a QR Code?

A QR code is a **square barcode** that looks like this:

```
[██████████████]
[█   ██     ██ █]
[██ █  ██  █  ██]
[█████████████]
```

It contains your **unique player ID**. The court scanner reads it and knows it's you!

### How to View Your QR Code

1. Login to your account
2. Click **"My QR Code"** in the menu
   OR
   From dashboard, click **"[View QR Code]"**
3. Your QR code appears on screen — **full screen display**

### Using Your QR Code to Play

**At the Court:**

1. **Show your phone screen** with the QR code
   - Make sure screen brightness is high (so it scans clearly)
   - Or print your QR code on a card (ask staff)

2. **Staff scans your QR code** with a barcode scanner
   - They'll point the scanner at your phone
   - Beep sound = scanned successfully

3. **System checks:**
   - ✅ Do you have ₱10+ credits? YES → You're added to queue!
   - ❌ Do you have ₱10+ credits? NO → "Insufficient balance. Please load credits."

4. **If approved:** 
   - You're added to the waiting list
   - Check the display screen to see your position
   - Wait for your turn to play

### Important QR Code Facts

✅ **Your QR code is permanent** — It never changes
✅ **Only YOU can use your code** — It's tied to your account
✅ **QR code expires in 8 hours** after you first load it (but renews when you reload)
❌ **Don't share your QR code** — It's your "key"
❌ **Screenshots don't work** — Only the live screen version works

### Troubleshooting QR Code

**"QR code won't scan"**
- Make sure phone screen is clean (wipe the glass)
- Increase screen brightness (swipe down from top)
- Try different angle (usually 45 degrees is best)
- Ask staff if scanner is working

**"QR code says 'expired'"**
- Go back to dashboard and click "View QR Code" again
- System will refresh your QR code

**"System says 'insufficient balance'"**
- You don't have ₱10+ in your wallet
- Go to **Top Up** to add more credits
- Come back after admin approves

## <a name="topup"></a>How to Load Credits (Top Up)

### Understanding Credits

Think of credits like **prepaid phone load**. Just like you buy load before calling, you buy credits before playing.

- **1 credit = ₱1**
- **Each game costs ₱10 credits** (e.g., 10 credits)
- **When game ends, ₱10 is deducted from your balance**

### Common Top-Up Amounts

| Amount | Good For | Price |
|--------|----------|-------|
| ₱50 | 5 games | ₱50 |
| ₱100 | 10 games | ₱100 |
| ₱200 | 20 games | ₱200 |
| ₱500 | 50 games | ₱500 |

### How to Top Up

#### Step 1: Go to Top-Up Page

1. Login to your account
2. Click **"Top Up"** in the menu
   OR
   From dashboard, click **"[Top Up]"** button

#### Step 2: Choose Payment Method

You'll see a list of payment options:

**💳 GCash**
- Send money to the court's GCash number
- Fastest (instant approval)

**🏦 Bank Transfer**
- Transfer from your bank app
- Usually approved same day

**💵 Cash at Counter**
- Pay in person at the court
- Get immediate credit

### Detailed Steps for Each Method

#### Method 1: GCash Top-Up

1. Select **"GCash"** from payment options
2. System shows:
   - Court's GCash number: `09171234567` (example)
   - GCash QR code to scan
   - How much to send: **₱[your choice]**

3. **On your phone:**
   - Open your **GCash app**
   - Tap **"Send Money"** or **"Pay"**
   - Scan the QR code OR enter the number
   - Enter amount (e.g., ₱100)
   - Confirm and send

4. **Take a screenshot** of the transfer receipt
   - Make sure it shows: GCash logo, amount (₱100), reference number
   - Keep this screenshot!

5. **Back in Falcon app:**
   - You'll see a form asking for proof
   - Enter **GCash Reference Number** (from your receipt, looks like: `P123456789X`)
   - Upload your screenshot
   - Click **"Submit"**

6. **Wait for approval:**
   - ⏳ Admin reviews your proof within a few minutes
   - 📬 You get notification when approved
   - ✅ Credits appear in your balance instantly!
   - ❌ If rejected, you'll get reason why (contact staff)

#### Method 2: Bank Transfer

1. Select **"Bank Transfer"** from payment options
2. System shows:
   - Bank name: BDO / BPI / etc.
   - Account name: "Falcon Pickleball Court"
   - Account number: `123456789`
   - Amount to transfer

3. **In your bank app:**
   - Tap "Transfer Money"
   - Select destination bank
   - Enter account number
   - Enter account name
   - Enter amount (₱100, ₱500, etc.)
   - Reference: "Your username" (e.g., "juan_dela_cruz")
   - Confirm

4. **Take a screenshot** of the confirmation
   - Shows: Bank, amount, reference, transfer time
   - Save the receipt!

5. **Back in Falcon app:**
   - Enter transfer details
   - Upload screenshot
   - Click **"Submit"**

6. **Wait for approval:**
   - ⏳ Usually approved within 24 hours (banks are slower)
   - 📬 Notification when ready
   - ✅ Credits added to your wallet

#### Method 3: Cash at Counter

1. Select **"Cash at Counter"** from payment options
2. Go to the **court** in person
3. Ask staff: "I want to top up ₱100"
4. **Give them cash** (₱50, ₱100, ₱500, etc.)
5. Get a **receipt** from staff
6. Staff enters it in system
7. ✅ Credits appear **immediately** in your account!

### Troubleshooting Top-Up

**"It's been 1 hour and still pending"**
- GCash requests usually approved within 5-10 minutes
- If longer: Admin might be offline (check if they're there)
- Contact staff directly or send message

**"Screenshot got rejected"**
- Amount didn't match? (You sent ₱50 but claimed ₱100)
- Reference number unclear?
- Receipt doesn't show GCash/bank logo?
- Ask admin why it was rejected
- Submit again with clearer photo

**"I forgot to take screenshot"**
- For GCash: Get the reference number from your transaction history in GCash app
- For Bank: Check your bank app's transfer history
- Send the reference number to admin (staff can verify)

## <a name="reservations"></a>How to Book a Court (Reservations)

### What is a Reservation?

Instead of **waiting in a queue**, you can **book a specific court at a specific time** — like booking a hotel room.

### Benefits of Reservations

✅ **Guaranteed court** — It's yours for that time
✅ **No waiting** — Show up, play immediately
✅ **Plan ahead** — Book days or weeks in advance
✅ **Great for tournaments** — Reserve for your team

### Before You Book

**Prerequisites:**
- You must have ✅ verified account (admin confirms you're real)
- You must have enough ₱ balance to cover deposit
- Reservation available for that date/time

### How to Book a Court

#### Step 1: Go to Schedule Page

1. Login to your account
2. Click **"Schedule"** in the menu
3. You'll see a **calendar grid** with time slots

#### Step 2: Pick Your Date

1. Use the calendar to select date
   - Click on a specific date
   - OR use arrow buttons to navigate weeks
   - ✅ Green dates = available days
   - ❌ Gray dates = court closed/no availability

#### Step 3: Select Your Time Slot

The grid shows **time slots** (usually 30-min intervals):

```
TIME    |  AVAILABLE  |  BOOKED  |
6:00 AM |   [Available] |
7:00 AM |   [Available] |
8:00 AM |   [Booked - juan_p] |
```

- **[Available]** — You can book this time
- **[Booked]** — Someone else has this time

Click on an **[Available]** slot to open booking form.

#### Step 4: Fill Booking Form

**Date & Time**
- Already filled from your selection
- Can't change (close form and pick different slot)

**Duration** (how long you want court)
- Select: 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, etc.
- Most people book 1-2 hours

**Party Size** (how many people)
- How many players: 2, 3, 4+
- Maximum allowed: Usually 4 (doubles)

**Contact Number**
- Your phone number
- Staff uses this if they need to reach you

**Notes** (optional)
- Special requests, e.g., "Birthday party, please set up balloons"

#### Step 5: Check Pricing

**Reservation Price Breakdown:**

```
Hourly Rate:        ₱150/hour
Duration:           2 hours
Subtotal:           ₱300

Deposit (50%):      ₱150
Full Amount Due:    ₱300
```

- **Subtotal** = hours × hourly rate
- **Deposit** = 50% of total (you pay now, rest on day of play)
- **Full amount due** = what you'll eventually pay

### Step 6: Confirm and Submit

1. Review all details one more time
2. Click **"Book Now"** button
3. ⏳ Status: "Pending Admin Approval"
4. Wait for admin to confirm

### After You Book

**Pending Status:**
```
🕐 RESERVATION PENDING
Date: April 30, 2026
Time: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM
Status: ⏳ Waiting for admin approval
```

- Admin usually approves within a few hours
- You'll get **notification** when approved

**Approved Status:**
```
✅ RESERVATION CONFIRMED
Date: April 30, 2026
Time: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM
Deposit Paid: ₱150
Remaining: ₱150 due on day of play
```

- Your reservation is confirmed!
- You can show up and play
- Remaining balance due before/during play

### On the Day of Your Reservation

1. **Arrive a few minutes early**
2. **Tell staff your name** or **scan your QR code**
3. Staff checks you off as "arrived"
4. **You're added to the active game**
5. Play!

### What If You Need to Cancel?

1. Go to **Schedule** → find your reservation
2. Click **"Cancel Reservation"**
3. ⚠️ **Cancellation Policy:**
   - Cancel 24+ hours before: ✅ Full refund
   - Cancel <24 hours: ❌ No refund
4. Click confirm
5. Your deposit is refunded (if applicable)

## <a name="open-play"></a>How to Play (Open Play / Walk-in)

### What is Open Play?

Open play is **casual play — no booking required**. You show up, scan your QR, and wait for your turn.

### Open Play vs Reservation

| Aspect | Open Play | Reservation |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Need to book? | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Just show up? | ✅ Yes | ❌ Must be on time |
| Wait for turn? | ✅ Queue up | ❌ Immediate |
| Best for | Casual walk-ins | Tournaments, groups |

### Open Play Hours

Check your court's schedule to see **when open play is available**:

- **Typical:** 8 PM - 11 PM nightly
- **Weekends:** Often all day
- **Check dashboard** to see today's mode

### Step-by-Step: How to Play

#### Step 1: Check You Have Enough Credits

1. Open your **dashboard** on your phone
2. Look at **"YOUR BALANCE"** widget
3. Need at least **₱10** to play
4. ❌ If balance is ₱0: Top up first! (See "How to Load Credits" above)

#### Step 2: Arrive at the Court

- Open play times: Usually 8 PM - close
- Just walk in! No appointment needed
- Tell staff: "I want to join open play"
- OR go directly to scanner kiosk

#### Step 3: Find the Scanner

At the court, you'll find a **kiosk display** (tablet or screen with:

```
[QR Scanner Display]
🎮 OPEN PLAY
Queue: 4 waiting
```

- Approach the scanner
- Have your phone ready with QR code visible

#### Step 4: Scan Your QR Code

1. Go to your phone's **QR Code** page
   - Menu → "My QR Code"
   - OR Dashboard → "[View QR Code]"

2. **Show the QR code** to the scanner
   - Hold phone facing scanner
   - Keep it steady
   - Make sure screen is bright

3. **Barcode scanner beeps** — Scan successful! ✅

4. **What happens next:**
   - System checks your balance
   - ✅ Balance ≥ ₱10? → Added to queue!
   - ❌ Balance < ₱10? → "Insufficient credits. Please load ₱50+."

#### Step 5: Find Your Position

The scanner display shows:

```
QUEUE (4 WAITING)
1. juan_garcia    ✓
2. maria_santos   ✓
3. carlos_reyes   ✓
4. YOU (anna_p)   ✓
```

- Find your name in the list
- Note your position number
- Green ✓ = verified (you're good to play)

#### Step 6: Wait for Your Turn

**Position #1-3 ahead of you?**
- They'll play their 60-minute game first
- Display shows countdown: "Current game: 45 min remaining"
- Rough wait time: 1 hour per person ahead

**What to do while waiting:**
- Grab water, snacks
- Chat with other players
- Stretch and warm up
- Watch current game

#### Step 7: Your Turn — Game Starts!

When you reach **Position #1:**

1. **Staff calls your name** or displays on screen
2. **Go to the court** with 3 other players
3. **Warm up** for a few minutes (5 min usually)
4. **Game starts!** — 60-minute timer begins
5. **Play pickleball!** 🎾

#### Step 8: Game Ends

After 60 minutes:

1. **System automatically ends game**
2. **₱10 credits deducted** from your balance
3. **You exit the court**
4. **Go back to scanner** and scan again if you want another game!
5. OR if queue is growing, new players take your spot

### Can't Stay for Your Turn?

**If you need to leave while queued:**

1. Tell staff: "I need to cancel my queue position"
2. Or go to scanner and ask staff to remove you
3. ✅ No penalty — you just leave
4. Your ₱10 credit stays in your account

## <a name="balance"></a>Understanding Your Balance

### What is Your Balance?

Your balance is the **amount of credits you have** — like money in a bank account.

```
💰 YOUR BALANCE: ₱250.00
```

- Each credit = ₱1
- Each game costs ₱10 credits
- ₱250 = enough for 25 games

### How Your Balance Changes

**Balance increases when:**
- ✅ You submit a top-up request
- ✅ Admin approves your top-up
- ✅ You pay cash at counter
- ✅ Admin gives you a bonus/credit
- ✅ Your game is cancelled (refund)

**Balance decreases when:**
- ❌ You complete a game (₱10 deducted)
- ❌ Admin adjusts your balance for dispute

### Low Balance Warning

When you're running low on credits:

- **⚠️ Alert at ₱10:** "Your balance is low. Consider topping up."
- **❌ ₱0:** You can't play anymore until you top up

### Checking Your Balance

**Method 1: Dashboard**
1. Login
2. Look at top-right: **"YOUR BALANCE: ₱XXX"**

**Method 2: Mobile Wallet Page**
1. Menu → "Wallet"
2. Shows: Current balance, recent transactions

**Method 3: QR Code Page**
1. Menu → "My QR Code"
2. Small text shows: "Balance: ₱250"

### Transaction History

**See where your money went:**

1. Go to Wallet page
2. Scroll down to **Transactions** section
3. Shows each debit/credit:

| Date | Type | Amount | Description |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Apr 22 | Debit | -₱10 | Game played — Court 1 |
| Apr 21 | Credit | +₱100 | Top-up approved (GCash) |
| Apr 20 | Debit | -₱10 | Game played — Court 2 |

**Debit:** Money went out (game played)
**Credit:** Money came in (top-up approved)

### Dispute a Transaction

Think there's a mistake?

1. Take a screenshot of the transaction
2. Contact staff with details:
   - Date and time
   - Amount
   - What you think is wrong
3. Staff can review and adjust if needed

## <a name="faq"></a>Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your phone number. You'll get a reset link via email or SMS.

**Q: Can I change my username?**
A: No, username is permanent. Choose carefully when registering!

**Q: My phone number has changed. Can I update it?**
A: Go to Profile → Edit Profile. You can update your phone number there.

**Q: What if I share my QR code with my friend?**
A: Don't! Each QR is unique to you. If someone uses your QR, credits are deducted from YOUR balance, not theirs.

**Q: Do I need to bring my physical QR card?**
A: No! Your phone screen works. You can print a QR card as backup.

**Q: Can I play without paying?**
A: No, you need ₱10 per game. You must have credits in your account.

**Q: What if a game is cancelled while I'm playing?**
A: If cancelled by admin for emergency (injury, weather), your ₱10 credit is refunded to your wallet.

**Q: How long does a game last?**
A: Usually 60 minutes, but court owner can set different durations (45, 90 min, etc.).

**Q: How many people play in a game?**
A: Usually 4 people (doubles: 2 vs 2). Some courts allow more.

**Q: What if I'm position #10 in queue?**
A: You'll wait about 2-3 hours (depends on game duration). You can leave and come back later, but you'll lose your position.

**Q: Can I reserve multiple courts at once?**
A: Yes! Book separate time slots for different courts/times.

**Q: What's the deposit for reservations?**
A: Usually 50% of total cost. You pay deposit when booking, rest on day of play.

**Q: Do I get a refund if I cancel my reservation?**
A: Only if you cancel ≥24 hours before. If you cancel <24 hours, no refund.

**Q: Can I transfer credits to another player?**
A: No, credits are personal to your account. You can't send/transfer.

**Q: What if I'm banned?**
A: You'll see "Your account was banned" when you try to login. Contact staff to appeal.

**Q: How do I verify my account?**
A: Usually you're automatically verified after registration. If not verified, ask staff.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Contact staff to request account deletion. Some data may be kept for auditing.

**Q: What happens to my credits if I delete my account?**
A: They're forfeited. Make sure to use your balance before deleting!

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes! The system uses encryption and secure servers. Your phone number and password are protected.

**Q: Can staff see my password?**
A: No, they can only reset it (give you a new temporary one). Your actual password is never visible to anyone.

**Q: What if there's a dispute about a charge?**
A: Contact staff with details. They can review the transaction and refund if needed.

**Q: Is there an age limit?**
A: Check with your court. Usually 13+ can play independently, younger kids need parental permission.

## Appendix: Common Errors & Solutions

### Error: "Invalid username or password"
- Check spelling (case-sensitive)
- Make sure CAPS LOCK is off
- Reset password if forgotten

### Error: "Insufficient balance"
- You don't have ₱10+
- Go to Top Up
- Submit payment request and wait for approval

### Error: "QR code expired"
- Refresh the page
- Go to "My QR Code" page again
- Your QR code will renew

### Error: "Account not verified"
- Contact staff
- They'll verify your account manually
- Try logging in again after

### Error: "Account suspended"
- Your account was banned
- You violated rules (violence, theft, etc.)
- Contact staff to appeal

### Error: "Database connection failed"
- System is temporarily down
- Refresh page (Ctrl+R)
- Try again in 5 minutes
- Contact support if still broken
